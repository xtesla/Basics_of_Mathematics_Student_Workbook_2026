# Exercise 9 - A System from a Sum Condition

## Problem Statement

Find numbers $x,y$ satisfying
```math
x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.
```
Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Theoretical Background and Concepts

Two independent linear equations in two unknowns represent lines meeting at one point.

## Step-by-Step Solution

From $x+y=10$, $x=10-y$. Substitute into $2x+5y=32$: $20-2y+5y=32$, so $y=4$ and $x=6$.

## Verification and Consistency Checks

$6+4=10$ and $2(6)+5(4)=32$. The slopes differ, so the intersection is unique.
